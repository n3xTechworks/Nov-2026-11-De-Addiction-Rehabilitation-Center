Privacy Policy

How Harborlight protects the personal and health information you share with us.

Last updated 1 November 2026

Our promise

Harborlight Recovery Centre (“Harborlight”, “we”) provides confidential treatment for people affected by alcohol, drugs, gambling and gaming. We know how sensitive this information is. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it. It applies to this website, our helpline, enquiries and the care we provide.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and to provide safe care:

  • Enquiry details: the name you give (a first name is fine), email, phone number, who the enquiry is for, your main concern, preferred contact method and time, and any message.
  • Clinical information: if you become a client, your health history, assessments, treatment notes and medicines.
  • Website data: basic, anonymous usage data and your theme and reading-direction preferences, stored only in your own browser.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry in the way you asked.
  • To assess, plan and provide treatment and aftercare.
  • To check insurance cover, only when you ask us to.
  • To meet legal duties, such as keeping medical records for the required period.

We never sell your information, use it for advertising, or send marketing messages about treatment.

Confidentiality and sharing

Your information is protected by medical confidentiality. We share it only with your written consent, for example with a family member you nominate, your own doctor or your insurer. The rare exceptions are when someone is at serious and immediate risk of harm, or when the law requires it, and even then we share only what is necessary.

Calls and messages from us come from a plain number and never mention addiction or rehabilitation.

How we protect it

Records are stored in encrypted systems with access limited to the clinicians involved in your care. Staff receive regular confidentiality training, and every access to clinical records is logged.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to act for you. Enquiries that do not lead to treatment are deleted after 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Contact our privacy officer

Email privacy@harborlight.example or write to the Privacy Officer, Harborlight Recovery Centre, 18 Lighthouse Lane, Kovalam Road, Muttukadu, Chennai 603112. We reply within 30 days.
