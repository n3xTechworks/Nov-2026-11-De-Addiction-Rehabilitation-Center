Recovery, together

Recovery is a team effort.

You do not have to work it out alone. Counsellors, doctors, peers and family walk each step with you, in complete confidence.

Start a confidential conversationSee how recovery works

You are welcome here

Whatever brought you here, you are in the right place

Reaching out is a sign of strength. We meet every person with respect, curiosity and hope.

Some people call about themselves; many call about a son, a partner or a parent. Either way, the first conversation is free, private and comes with no obligation.

The recovery journey

Four steps, at a pace that feels right

Recovery is not a straight line. These are the stages most people move through with us, and you can pause, repeat or return to any of them.

  1. 1

    A first private call

    Talk to a counsellor on our 24/7 helpline about what is happening. No names needed.

  2. 2

    Assessment and a plan

    A doctor and counsellor meet you to understand your health, history and goals.

  3. 3

    Treatment that fits

    Detox, residential or day therapy, using CBT, DBT, MAT and mindfulness where they help.

  4. 4

    Aftercare and everyday life

    A year of weekly groups, reviews and a named contact as you settle back into life.

Family support

When someone you love is struggling, you need support too

Families carry worry, guilt and exhaustion. Our family program gives you a place to be heard and practical ways to help without losing yourself.

  • Weekly family therapyGuided sessions with a family therapist to rebuild trust and talk openly.
  • Saturday family circleA free peer group for parents, partners and adult children, in person or online.
  • Sunday visitingFrom the second week of a residential stay, 10:00 to 16:00 in the gardens.

Daily structure

A calm rhythm that helps the days hold together

Routine lowers stress and rebuilds sleep, appetite and focus. A residential day is full, but never rushed.

Morning07:00 to 12:00

Wake gently, then therapy

A garden walk or guided breathing, breakfast together, then a CBT group on triggers and cravings.

  • Mindfulness, group therapy

Midday12:00 to 14:30

One-to-one time

A session with your named counsellor, lunch, and quiet time to rest, read or call home.

  • Individual counselling

Afternoon14:30 to 18:00

Skills and movement

DBT skills, art or music therapy, then yoga, the gym or a game of badminton.

  • DBT, creative therapy, yoga

Evening18:00 to 22:00

Connection and reflection

Dinner, an optional 12-step or SMART Recovery meeting, journaling and lights out by ten.

  • Peer support, journaling
