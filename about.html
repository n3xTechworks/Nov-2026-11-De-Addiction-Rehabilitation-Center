About Harborlight

Our story and approach

A calm, confidential place to recover, founded on dignity, evidence and family.

Our story

It began with one family who had nowhere to turn

In 2012 Dr Leela Krishnan, a psychiatrist in Chennai, kept meeting families who wanted help for someone they loved but found only locked wards or long waiting lists. She opened Harborlight in a rented seaside house with six beds and one promise: treat every person with dignity.

Today our four-acre campus at Muttukadu and our city counselling office in R. A. Puram have supported more than 2,400 people and their families, and that promise has not changed.

  • Dignity firstWe see a person, never a label. Nobody here is called an addict.
  • Private by defaultNothing is shared with employers or family without written consent.

Our approach

Evidence-based care, shaped around one person at a time

Substance use is a health condition, not a moral failing. We combine medical care, proven talking therapies and practical life skills, and we adjust the plan as you change.

  • A full medical and psychiatric assessment before any plan is made
  • A named counsellor who stays with you from admission to aftercare
  • Families involved from week one, with your consent

How we help

Six approaches with strong evidence behind them

Your plan draws on the ones that suit you. 12-step and SMART Recovery meetings are offered too, and are always optional.

Talking therapy

Cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT)

Notice the thoughts and situations that lead to use, and practise new, workable responses.

Skills

Dialectical behaviour therapy (DBT)

Skills for strong emotions, distress tolerance and calmer, steadier relationships.

Medical

Medication-assisted treatment (MAT)

Safe, prescribed medicines that ease withdrawal and cravings, reviewed by our psychiatrists.

Wellbeing

Mindfulness and yoga

Daily practice that lowers stress, improves sleep and helps people ride out cravings.

Family

Family therapy

Guided sessions that rebuild trust and prepare everyone for life after treatment.

Long term

Relapse prevention

A written plan for high-risk moments, early warning signs and who to call.

Milestones

Fourteen years of steady growth

Every step was shaped by the people we support and the families who stand beside them.

  1. 2012

    Harborlight opens

    Six beds in a seaside house at Muttukadu, a psychiatrist, two counsellors and a nurse.

  2. 2015

    Family program begins

    Weekly family therapy and a free Saturday circle for parents, partners and children.

  3. 2018

    The new campus

    A purpose-built 30 bed campus with gardens, a yoga hall and an on-site medical unit.

  4. 2020

    24/7 helpline and online care

    A toll-free confidential helpline and secure video therapy for people across India.

  5. 2023

    City counselling office

    Day programs and aftercare groups open in R. A. Puram for people who keep working.

  6. 2026

    Digital wellbeing program

    Specialist support for gaming, gambling and phone use that has taken over daily life.

Standards and safety

Licensed, accountable and transparent

We follow India's mental health law and recognised clinical standards, and we are inspected every year.

Registered establishment

Registered with the State Mental Health Authority under the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017.

Clinical guidelines

Treatment follows national guidelines for substance use disorders and is reviewed each quarter.

Confidential records

Secure, access-controlled records. Nothing is shared without your written consent.

Qualified team

Psychiatrists, psychologists and nurses with ongoing training in trauma-informed care.
