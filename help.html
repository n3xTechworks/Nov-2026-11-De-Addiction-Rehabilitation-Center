Admission and assessment

How do I start?

Call the 24/7 helpline on 1800 270 4455 or send a confidential enquiry from our Contact page. A counsellor will talk through what is happening and arrange a free assessment, in person or by video.

What happens at the assessment?

A doctor and a counsellor spend about an hour with you, asking about your health, substance use, mood, family and goals. Together you agree which program fits best. There is no obligation to go ahead.

How soon can I be admitted?

Usually within 24 to 72 hours of the assessment. If medically supervised detox is needed urgently, our doctors will advise on the safest way to begin, which may be the same day.

Do I need a referral from a doctor?

No. Most people contact us themselves or through a family member. If you do have a doctor or psychiatrist, we are glad to work with them, with your permission.

Can someone be admitted against their wishes?

No. Harborlight is a voluntary centre for adults aged 18 and over, and every resident chooses to be here. If a loved one is not ready, our family team can help you with ways to encourage them.

Confidentiality and privacy

Will my employer find out?

Not from us. We never contact employers without your written consent. If you need a medical certificate, it can simply say you were receiving treatment for a health condition.

Who can see my records?

Only the clinicians involved in your care. Records are stored securely and protected by medical confidentiality, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Can my family get updates?

Only if you agree, and only the information you choose. Many residents nominate one family member for weekly updates.

Are phones allowed?

Phones are kept safely by staff in the first week so you can settle in, then returned for evening calls. Internet access is limited to protect everyone's privacy.

Programs and treatment

Which program is right for me?

That depends on your health, your home situation and your goals. Residential care suits people who need a break from their surroundings; outpatient programs suit those who can stay safely at home. Your assessment will help you decide.

What therapies do you use?

Evidence-based approaches including CBT, DBT skills, motivational interviewing, mindfulness-based relapse prevention, family therapy and, where helpful, medication-assisted treatment. 12-step and SMART Recovery meetings are optional.

Do you treat anxiety or depression as well?

Yes. Many people live with both a substance use disorder and a mental health condition. Our dual diagnosis program treats both together with a psychiatrist and psychologist.

What happens after I leave?

Every person leaves with a written relapse-prevention plan and a place in our aftercare program: weekly groups, check-ins and an alumni community for at least a year.

Cost and insurance

How much does treatment cost?

Residential care starts from ₹1,45,000 for 28 days, all-inclusive. Medically supervised detox starts from ₹42,000 for 7 days, outpatient programs from ₹38,000 for 8 weeks and aftercare from ₹4,500 a month.

Does insurance cover treatment?

Many Indian health insurers now cover part of inpatient treatment for substance use disorders. Our admissions team checks your policy for free and in confidence.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. We offer interest-free instalments for residential and outpatient programs. Ask our admissions team for details.

Visiting and what to bring

When can family visit?

On Sundays from 10:00 to 16:00, from the second week of a residential stay. Families also join a weekly family therapy session in person or by video.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes for about a week (we have laundry), walking shoes, toiletries without alcohol, any current medicines in their original packs, ID and your insurance details.

What should I leave at home?

Alcohol, non-prescribed medicines, valuables, and products that contain alcohol such as some mouthwashes and perfumes. Staff will check bags gently and respectfully on arrival.
