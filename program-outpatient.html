Core program

Outpatient day program

Structured therapy three to five days a week, while you keep living at home and working.

Who it is for

Is the day program right for you?

Intensive support that fits around work, study and family. You attend morning or evening sessions and practise new skills in real life between them.

  • People with a stable home and a supportive person nearby
  • Anyone stepping down from residential care or detox
  • Working adults and students who cannot take weeks away
  • People who want help early, before problems grow

What a day looks like

A typical session day

Morning track shown. The evening track follows the same pattern from 18:00.

  1. 08:30

    Check-in

    A short one-to-one check on mood, cravings and the week ahead.

  2. 09:00

    Group therapy

    CBT and relapse-prevention work with a small group of peers.

  3. 10:30

    Skills workshop

    Practical DBT and mindfulness skills you can use the same day.

  4. 11:30

    Counselling or family session

    Weekly individual counselling, with family sessions twice a month.

  5. 12:30

    Plan for the week

    Agree one small goal to practise before your next visit.

Therapies we use

Evidence-based, and built around you

Every plan is personal. These are the approaches most people in this program find helpful.

  1. 1

    Cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT)

    Practical work on triggers, cravings and high-risk situations.

  2. 2

    Motivational interviewing

    Respectful conversations that strengthen your own reasons for change.

  3. 3

    Medication-assisted treatment (MAT)

    Where suitable, prescribed medicines that reduce cravings, reviewed by our psychiatrist.

  4. 4

    Family sessions

    Help for partners and parents to support recovery without taking control.

Questions

Common questions

Anything else? Ask us in confidence. There is no obligation.

Are there evening sessions?

Yes. An evening track runs 18:00 to 21:00 on weekdays for people who work during the day.

Will my employer find out?

No. We do not contact employers. If you need a medical certificate, it can state a health condition without naming addiction.

What if I relapse during the program?

A slip is treated as information, not failure. Your counsellor adjusts the plan and, if needed, discusses a short residential stay.

Can I start right away?

Most people start within a week of their first assessment.
