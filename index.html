You are welcome here

Recovery is possible, and it does not have to wait

You do not need to hit rock bottom to ask for help. One honest conversation is often the first step back to the life you want.

Harborlight is a calm, private campus by the sea where people in recovery are treated with dignity. Our psychiatrists, psychologists, nurses and counsellors use proven therapies, and every plan is built around the person, not a label.

Medical support

Safe, dignified care from the very first day

Every admission begins with an unhurried assessment by a doctor. If your body needs help to withdraw, our medical team keeps you safe and comfortable around the clock.

  • Psychiatrist-led assessmentPhysical health, mental health and goals, in one conversation.
  • Medically supervised detoxProven medicines to ease withdrawal, with nurses on site 24/7.
  • Care for co-occurring conditionsDepression, anxiety and trauma treated alongside addiction.

Rehabilitation support

Therapy that rebuilds routines, relationships and hope

Recovery is more than stopping. With a named counsellor, small groups and creative therapies, you learn to handle cravings, stress and difficult days in new ways.

  • CBT, DBT and mindfulnessPractical skills you practise every day, not just talk about.
  • Family therapy every weekRepair trust and prepare everyone for life at home.
  • A year of aftercareWeekly groups and a counsellor on call after you leave.
