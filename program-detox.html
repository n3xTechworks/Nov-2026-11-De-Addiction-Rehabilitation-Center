Core program

Medically supervised detox

A safe, comfortable withdrawal with doctors and nurses beside you, day and night.

Who it is for

Is medical detox right for you?

Withdrawal from alcohol and some drugs can be dangerous without medical care. Our detox unit keeps you safe, eases symptoms with medication and starts therapy as soon as you feel ready.

  • People who drink heavily every day or have had shakes, sweats or seizures when stopping
  • People dependent on opioids, sedatives or sleeping tablets
  • Anyone who has tried to stop alone and found withdrawal too hard
  • People preparing to join residential or outpatient therapy

What a day looks like

What the detox days look like

Everyone withdraws differently. Your doctor adjusts the plan to how you feel each day.

  1. Day 1

    Assessment and settling in

    A full medical check, blood tests and a personal medication plan.

  2. Days 2 to 3

    Close monitoring

    Nurses check vital signs every few hours and adjust medicines to keep you comfortable.

  3. Days 4 to 5

    Feeling steadier

    Sleep and appetite return. Gentle counselling and short walks begin.

  4. Days 6 to 10

    Planning the next step

    You meet the therapy team and choose residential, outpatient or aftercare support.

Therapies we use

Evidence-based, and built around you

Every plan is personal. These are the approaches most people in this program find helpful.

  1. 1

    Medication-assisted withdrawal

    Evidence-based medicines that prevent complications and ease symptoms.

  2. 2

    Nutrition and rest

    Vitamins, fluids and simple, nourishing meals to help the body recover.

  3. 3

    Motivational counselling

    Short, supportive conversations as soon as you feel ready.

  4. 4

    Family briefing

    A call with your family to explain what to expect and how to help.

Questions

Common questions

Anything else? Ask us in confidence. There is no obligation.

Is detox painful?

Our doctors use proven medicines to keep withdrawal as comfortable and safe as possible. Most people say it was far easier than stopping alone.

Is detox enough on its own?

Detox treats the body. Lasting recovery needs therapy afterwards, which is why every detox includes a plan for the next step.

Can someone stay with me?

A family member may visit daily. In some cases, a relative can stay for the first night.

Do you treat young adults?

We admit adults aged 18 and over. For younger people we will refer you to a trusted adolescent service.
