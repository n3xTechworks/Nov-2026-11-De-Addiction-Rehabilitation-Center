Core program

Residential rehabilitation

Live on our quiet campus for 28 to 90 days, with round-the-clock care and a full daily therapy plan.

Who it is for

Is residential care right for you?

Time away from daily triggers, in a safe and structured setting. Each resident has a private or twin room, a named counsellor and a plan built around their goals.

  • People whose drinking or drug use has not improved with outpatient support
  • Anyone who needs distance from a home or work setting that keeps them using
  • People who have completed detox and want structure while they rebuild routines
  • Adults of any age, with or without a co-occurring mental health condition

What a day looks like

A typical day on campus

Days are structured but never rushed, with time to rest, reflect and call home.

  1. 07:00

    Wake, walk and mindfulness

    A gentle start: a garden walk or guided breathing before breakfast.

  2. 09:30

    Group therapy

    CBT-based group work on triggers, cravings and thinking patterns.

  3. 11:30

    One-to-one counselling

    Time with your named counsellor to work on your personal plan.

  4. 14:30

    Skills and creative therapy

    Art, music or DBT skills sessions that build new ways to cope.

  5. 17:00

    Yoga, gym or sport

    Movement to rebuild sleep, appetite and energy.

  6. 19:30

    Peer meeting and reflection

    An optional 12-step or SMART Recovery meeting, then journaling.

Therapies we use

Evidence-based, and built around you

Every plan is personal. These are the approaches most people in this program find helpful.

  1. 1

    Cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT)

    Spot the thoughts and situations that lead to use, and practise new responses.

  2. 2

    Dialectical behaviour therapy (DBT) skills

    Tools for strong emotions, distress tolerance and calmer relationships.

  3. 3

    Mindfulness and yoga

    Daily practice that lowers stress and helps you ride out cravings.

  4. 4

    Family therapy

    Weekly sessions that repair trust and prepare everyone for the return home.

Questions

Common questions

Anything else? Ask us in confidence. There is no obligation.

Can my family visit?

Yes. Families visit on Sundays from the second week, and join a family therapy session each week. Calls are possible every evening.

Can I keep my phone?

Phones are kept safe during the first week so you can settle in, then returned for set hours each day.

Is my stay confidential?

Completely. We never confirm to anyone that you are a resident without your written consent, and records are kept under Indian mental health law.

What happens when I leave?

Every resident leaves with an aftercare plan, weekly follow-up sessions for a year and a named contact they can call any time.
