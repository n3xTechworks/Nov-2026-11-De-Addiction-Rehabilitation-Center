Questions

Before you get in touch

Common questions about confidentiality, admission, cost and visiting.

Is my enquiry really confidential?

Yes. Enquiries are read only by our clinical admissions team and are protected by medical confidentiality. We do not share anything with employers, insurers or family members without your written consent, unless someone is at serious and immediate risk.

Can I enquire on behalf of someone else?

Of course. Many enquiries come from parents, partners and friends. We can talk about how to raise the subject, what support exists for you, and what to do if your loved one is not ready yet.

How quickly can someone be admitted?

After a free assessment, admission is often possible within 24 to 72 hours. If medical detox is needed urgently, our doctors will advise on the safest way to start, which may be the same day.

How much does treatment cost?

Residential care starts from ₹1,45,000 for 28 days, all-inclusive, and outpatient programs from ₹38,000 for 8 weeks. Many health insurers cover part of treatment, and our team checks your policy for free.

Can family visit during a residential stay?

Yes. Families visit on Sundays from 10:00 to 16:00 from the second week, and join weekly family therapy sessions. Residents can also call home every evening.

Do I have to stop using before I call?

No. Please do not stop alcohol or some medicines suddenly on your own, as withdrawal can be dangerous. Call us as you are, and we will help you plan a safe next step.
