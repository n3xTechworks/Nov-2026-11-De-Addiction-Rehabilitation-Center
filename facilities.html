Facilities

A calm campus by the sea

Private rooms, four acres of gardens, spaces for therapy and movement, and round-the-clock medical care.

Our campus

Somewhere quiet to rest, think and begin again

Harborlight sits on four acres of gardens at Muttukadu, a short drive south of Chennai, close enough for family visits and far enough from daily pressures.

Every space was designed with people in recovery: soft light, private places to talk, room to walk, and no locked wards. Select any photo to see it full screen, or book a private tour with our admissions team.

Accommodation

Rooms that feel like a home, not a ward

Choose a private room or a twin-share room. Both have their own bathroom, fresh linen every few days and a window onto the gardens.

Private roomSingle occupancy

Private room

A quiet space of your own, with a writing desk, reading lamp and blackout curtains for better sleep. Ideal if you value privacy or need extra rest.

  • Queen bed and en-suite bathroom
  • Garden-facing window
  • Lockable storage and desk

From ₹1,80,000 for 28 days, all-inclusive

Twin-shareTwo residents

Twin-share room

Many residents find quiet company helpful. We match roommates carefully by age, gender and stage of recovery, and you can ask to move at any time.

  • Two single beds and en-suite bathroom
  • Glass doors onto a garden patio
  • Personal wardrobe for each resident

From ₹1,45,000 for 28 days, all-inclusive

Fees include meals, therapy, medical review and family sessions. See the residential program for details.

Wellness

Spaces to move, breathe and sleep well again

Gentle movement rebuilds appetite, sleep and mood. Every resident has a weekly plan with our yoga and wellness team.

Yoga and mindfulness studio

Morning yoga, breathing practice and guided mindfulness in a calm, sunlit hall. Every class is beginner-friendly.

  • Daily 07:00 and 17:00

Fitness room

Treadmills, bikes and light weights, with a physiotherapist on hand to build a safe programme around your health.

  • Open 06:00 to 20:00

Gardens and walking trail

Four acres of shaded paths, flower beds and quiet benches. Many residents say their best conversations happen here.

  • Open sunrise to sunset

Therapy and creative spaces

Rooms for honest conversations and new ways to express them

Alongside six private counselling rooms and two group rooms, our art and music studio gives people another way to work through feelings that are hard to put into words.

  • Counselling roomsSoundproofed, softly lit and never overlooked.
  • Group therapy roomsCircles of eight to ten for CBT, DBT skills and relapse prevention.
  • Art and music studioPainting, clay, journaling and music, led by a trained creative therapist.
How therapy fits into each program

Dining

Fresh, regular meals that help the body heal

Our nutritionist plans a weekly menu of home-style South Indian and North Indian dishes, with plenty of vegetables, protein and fruit. Meals are shared at long tables, which quietly rebuilds the rhythm of a day.

  • Three meals and two snacks every day
  • Vegetarian, Jain, diabetic and allergy-aware options
  • No caffeine after 15:00 to protect sleep

Medical care

A medical wing that keeps you safe, day and night

Our six-bed medical wing supports safe, comfortable detox and any health needs during your stay. Nurses are on site at all hours, and a doctor is always on call.

  • 24/7 nursingRegular checks, medicines and comfort through withdrawal.
  • Daily doctor roundsPsychiatric and physical health reviews, including medication-assisted treatment.
  • Hospital linksAgreements with two multi-speciality hospitals within 25 minutes.

Daily schedule

A day on campus

Structure brings calm. A typical weekday follows a steady rhythm, with free time built in and quieter weekends.

  1. 07:00

    Rise and mindfulness

    A garden walk or a short guided practice in the yoga studio.

  2. 08:15

    Breakfast together

    A warm breakfast in the dining room, followed by medicines and nurse check-ins.

  3. 09:30

    Group therapy

    CBT and relapse-prevention groups in small, consistent circles.

  4. 11:30

    One-to-one time

    Counselling, doctor reviews or family sessions, depending on your plan.

  5. 13:00

    Lunch and rest

    A shared lunch, then quiet time to read, nap or call home.

  6. 14:30

    Creative and skills sessions

    Art, music or DBT skills work in the studio.

  7. 17:00

    Movement

    Yoga, the fitness room or a walk around the gardens.

  8. 19:30

    Peer meeting and reflection

    An optional 12-step or SMART Recovery meeting, then journaling and wind-down. Lights dim at 22:00.
