Questions

Questions about our programs

If yours is not here, ask us in confidence. There is no obligation.

How do I know which program is right?

You do not need to decide alone. A free assessment with a doctor and counsellor looks at your health, history and daily life, and we recommend the gentlest program that is likely to work.

Can I keep working during treatment?

Yes. The outpatient day program has morning and evening tracks, and aftercare, family and digital programs all fit around work. We never contact your employer.

Is treatment covered by insurance?

Many Indian health insurers now cover detox and residential care for substance use. Our admissions team checks your policy for free and confidentially, and explains any remaining cost in rupees before you start.

Do I have to attend 12-step meetings?

No. 12-step and SMART Recovery meetings are offered and many people find them helpful, but they are always optional. Your plan is built on evidence-based therapies such as CBT, DBT and relapse prevention.

What if I relapse after treatment?

A lapse is common and is not a failure. Call your aftercare counsellor or the 24/7 helpline, and we will review your plan together, without judgement.
